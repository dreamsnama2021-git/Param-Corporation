Privacy Policy

1Who we are

Param Corporation ("Param", "we", "us", "our") designs, manufactures and delivers customised promotional merchandise, doctor engagement tools, patient education products and digital healthcare communication solutions for pharmaceutical and healthcare brands. Our registered office is at 101, Ketki Pada Road, near Timber Green Apartments, Diamond Industrial Estate, Dahisar East, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400068, India.

This policy applies to information collected through www.paramcorp.in, our enquiry channels (phone, email, WhatsApp), our social media pages, and the products and services we provide. It is written in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry and contact details: your name, company name, designation, email address, phone number and the message you send through our website, email or WhatsApp.
  • Order and business details: billing and shipping addresses, GST details, purchase orders, product briefs, artwork and brand files.
  • Communication records: emails, chat messages and call notes exchanged while we plan, design and deliver your project.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, IP address, approximate location, pages visited, time spent and referring website.
  • Information collected through cookies and similar technologies, described in section 7.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data such as health records, financial account passwords or biometric information through our website.

3Personalisation data

Many of our products, such as name stands, personalised desk utilities, QR-based gifts, photo frames and star maps, are customised for individual healthcare professionals. To make them, our clients may share data about doctors and other third parties, including names, clinic or hospital names, qualifications, photographs, addresses and phone numbers.

When a pharmaceutical company or other client shares this data with us, the client decides why it is collected and is responsible for having a lawful basis and any required consent to share it. Param processes it only on the client's instructions and only to produce and deliver the ordered products.

We do not use personalisation data for our own marketing, and we do not combine it with other datasets.

4Digital engagement tools

Some solutions we build, such as DigiPRO, QR code products and health risk assessment (HRA), EDD or ovulation calculators, may be used by doctors and patients. Where these tools are hosted or operated by Param:

  • Calculator inputs are used only to display the result on screen, unless the tool clearly tells the user otherwise.
  • We may collect anonymous usage statistics, such as scan counts or page views, to report campaign performance to our client.
  • Any tool that stores personal or health information will show its own notice and request consent before collecting it.

5How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and send quotations, samples and catalogues.
  • To design, manufacture, personalise, quality-check and deliver your orders across India.
  • To issue invoices, process payments and meet tax and accounting requirements.
  • To provide after-sales support and keep you informed about your project.
  • To send product updates or new catalogues, which you can opt out of at any time.
  • To improve our website, products and services, and to prevent fraud or misuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

6Sharing and disclosure

We never sell or rent personal information. We share it only when needed, and only with:

  • Production and logistics partners: printers, fabricators, packaging vendors and courier companies who need it to make and deliver your order.
  • Service providers: website hosting, cloud storage, email, analytics and payment providers working on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Authorities: government or law enforcement bodies when required by law, court order or to protect our legal rights.
  • Business transfers: a successor entity in case of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

Some service providers may store data outside India. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure the data stays protected as required by Indian law.

7Cookies and third parties

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep it working, remember preferences and understand how visitors use it. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some parts of the site may not work as expected.

Our website includes embedded content and links from third parties, such as our Instagram feed, WhatsApp chat links, and pages on Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube and Pinterest. These services may set their own cookies and collect information under their own privacy policies, which we do not control. We encourage you to read their policies before interacting with them.

8Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes described above. Enquiry details are kept for as long as we have an active business relationship or a reasonable prospect of one. Order, invoice and tax records are kept for the period required under Indian tax and company law. Personalisation data supplied for a specific order is deleted or anonymised once the order is delivered and any reprint or warranty period ends, unless the client asks us to keep it for repeat orders.

9Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. These include access controls, secure storage of client files, and confidentiality obligations for our staff and vendors. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will notify affected individuals and authorities of any data breach as required by law.

10Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Ask us to correct, complete or update inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your data when it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected.
  • Withdraw consent you have given, such as for marketing messages.
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us, and if it remains unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

If you are a doctor whose details were shared with us by a pharmaceutical client, you can contact us directly and we will coordinate with the client to address your request.

11Children's privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and healthcare professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or the law. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be highlighted on our website.

13Contact and grievances

For questions about this policy, or to exercise any of your rights, contact our Grievance Officer. We aim to acknowledge requests within 48 hours and resolve them within 30 days.
